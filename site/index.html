Dear whoever opens this

Thanks for opening the envelope.

I'm Pax. I carry two cameras: a film camera for the moments I'm willing to wait for, and a Canon EOS M50 Mark II for everything that won't wait.

Roll 001 is exactly what came back from the lab: Madrid in spring, a boulevard in Paris, and a last frame on a beach at dusk.

The prints cover five years of trips: Greece in 2021, Morocco in September 2025, Paris in February. If you're curious how the light was, the settings are under every print.
